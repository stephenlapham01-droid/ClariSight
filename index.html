Why ClariSight

Enrichment built
on principles

We made deliberate choices about what data to use and what to avoid. Those choices make the output trustworthy.

Public-source only

Every data point comes from company websites, open registries, licensed APIs, and public job boards. No scraped contacts, no gray-market databases.

Company-level focus

We enrich businesses, not individuals. No personal emails, no cell phones, no contact-data compliance risk. Better targeting without the privacy concerns.

Confidence-scored

Every record carries a transparent quality rating. We tell you what we found, what we couldn't verify, and what to double-check. Honest data, not fabricated filler.
